# PatteForm — Pitch Document
## Digital presence for every animal shelter

---

### The problem

France has over **3,500 animal shelters and rescue associations** (SPAs, ASPAs, fourrières, associations loi 1901). Most of them have:

- **No website** — or a free template from 2012
- **No online adoption catalog** — adopters call by phone or come in person
- **No digital admin tools** — animals managed on paper or in Excel

Meanwhile, **100,000+ animals are abandoned each year** in France (CNPA data). Digital visibility directly impacts adoption rates — shelters that showcase their animals online adopt out significantly more.

The barrier isn’t willingness — it’s capacity. A small ASPA chapter in rural France cannot afford a web agency quote of €5,000–15,000 for a custom site.

---

### The solution: PatteForm

PatteForm is a **free, open-source, self-hostable platform** that gives any animal shelter:

- A **complete public website** (adoption listings, news, contact, team page)
- A **full admin dashboard** (no code required)
- **White-label branding** (logo, colors, name — looks like YOUR shelter’s site)
- **Deployment in under 30 minutes** on any €2/month shared host

It’s the “WordPress for animal shelters” — built specifically for the needs and budget constraints of rescue organizations.

---

### Features that matter to decision-makers

| Feature | Why it matters |
|---------|----------------|
| **Self-hostable** | Data never leaves your server. RGPD compliant by design. |
| **No vendor lock-in** | Open source (GPL). No monthly SaaS fee. |
| **Accessible UI** | Designed for non-technical staff and volunteers |
| **Low hosting cost** | Standard PHP shared hosting (€2–5/month) |
| **Admin dashboard** | Add/edit animals, publish news, manage visits — no dev needed |
| **Adoption workflow** | Track animals from arrival → adoption, with visit scheduling |
| **Donation integration** | HelloAsso, PayPal, Hellodon — configurable link |
| **Mobile-responsive** | Works on phones — critical for field volunteers |

### Target institutions

#### 1. Communes & Intercommunalités
French law (article L211-24 du Code rural) requires communes to manage stray animals. Many work with local SPAs. PatteForm gives the commune + SPA a shared digital tool for:
- Displaying found/unclaimed animals online
- Managing owner reclaim requests
- Publishing adoption campaigns

**Pitch angle**: *“Un outil digital clé-en-main, conforme, déployé en une journée, hébergé pour moins d’un café par mois.”*

#### 2. Réseau SPA / ASPA (chapitres régionaux)
There are ~60 independent SPA chapters across France. A federation-level agreement would give every chapter access to PatteForm with:
- Central support
- Template branding per chapter
- Shared feature roadmap

**Pitch angle**: *“Une plateforme pour tout le réseau. Chaque section garde sa propre identité visuelle, sans frais par siège.”*

#### 3. DDPP (Directions Départementales de la Protection des Populations)
DDPPs oversee animal welfare compliance at the département level. A PatteForm partnership could:
- Provide standardized digital infrastructure to supervised associations
- Create a reporting module (intake, adoption rates, euthanasia data) for DDPP oversight
- Potentially qualify for subvention via FEADER/FNADT rural digital tools programs

**Pitch angle**: *“Donnez à chaque association que vous supervisez une plateforme conforme. Récupérez des données standardisées en retour.”*

#### 4. Fondation Brigitte Bardot / 30 Millions d’Amis
Both foundations fund technology projects that improve animal welfare at scale. PatteForm as an open platform serving hundreds of shelters is a strong fit for a **digital impact grant** (€10,000–50,000 range).

### Business model options

PatteForm is free and open source. Viable models for sustainability:

| Model | Description | Target |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| **Managed hosting** | Host PatteForm for shelters at €15–30/month/instance | Small/medium associations |
| **Setup package** | One-time €200–500 for installation + onboarding | Associations with own hosting |
| **Municipal contract** | Annual €500–2,000 for deployment + maintenance | Mairies |
| **Federation deal** | One-time setup + annual maintenance per network | FNSPA, UNCA, etc. |
| **Grant-funded** | ADEME / Fondation Bardot / 30 Millions d’Amis grants | Public funders |

The open-source model means zero marginal cost to serve one more shelter — the value you sell is **time, support, and reliability**, not the software.

---

### How to get started

1. **Pilot program** — offer 3–5 local shelters a free setup in exchange for feedback and testimonials
2. **Case study** — document adoption rate changes after 3 months online
3. **Approach the FNSPA** — one meeting with their digital committee could open the door to the whole network
4. **Grant application** — Fondation Brigitte Bardot has a spring grant cycle; PatteForm fits their “digital tools for rescue” category perfectly
